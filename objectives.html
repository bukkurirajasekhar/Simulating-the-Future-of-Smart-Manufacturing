What is a Smart Factory Monitor?

A virtual replica of a physical machine that receives real-time sensor data to simulate, monitor, and predict behavior — without disrupting the real machine.

🏭

Smart Manufacturing

Factories where machines, sensors, and humans communicate in real-time to automatically detect faults, reduce waste, and improve quality.

🤖

AI + Simulation

Machine learning models (LSTM, Random Forest) predict failures 48 hours in advance. Auto-simulation adjusts itself continuously as conditions change.

Problem Statement
Current manufacturing systems face critical challenges that this project solves.
⏱️

High Machine Downtime

Indian MSME industries experience 15–20% average downtime due to unexpected failures. Reactive maintenance causes production delays and increased costs.

⚡

Inefficient Energy Consumption

Manufacturing facilities waste energy during idle time and non-optimized processes. Lack of real-time monitoring makes it impossible to identify excessive usage.

🔮

Poor Predictive Capabilities

70% of maintenance is reactive — done only after a failure occurs. Without analytics and ML, factories cannot forecast equipment failures or bottlenecks.
